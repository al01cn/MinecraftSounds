# About MinecraftSounds

## Project Introduction

MinecraftSounds Music Pack Generator is a music pack generation tool designed specifically for Minecraft players, aimed at enabling players to easily create, manage, and share custom game sound effects and music. Our goal is to allow everyone, regardless of programming experience, to create professional music packs.

## Core Members

- **小阿狼是也 (XiaoALangShiYe)** - Project Lead & Main Programmer

## Project History

The MinecraftSounds Music Pack Generator project began in early August 2025, when we noticed that many players wanted to play their own music or audio but struggled as beginners who didn't know how to manually create music packs and lacked simple, easy-to-use tools.

Taking three days (August 10-13, 2025), the first test version of MinecraftSounds Music Pack Generator was developed by 小阿狼是也 and produced by 零一狼AL01.

## Technology Stack

MinecraftSounds Music Pack Generator is built using the following technologies:

- **Python** - Programming language used for the software
- **PyQt5** - Used for building the user interface
- **FFmpeg** - Used for audio processing
- **PyInstaller** - Used for packaging the software
- **Documentation website**: VitePress
