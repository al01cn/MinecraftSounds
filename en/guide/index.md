# MinecraftSounds Music Pack Generator Introduction

This generator was created after discovering that many beginners want to play their own music or sound effects in Minecraft.

The software is built with Python and uses pyqt5 for the interface, with AI-assisted development.  
The UI design features a Minecraft-style interface, making it very convenient to operate.

## What is a Music Pack?

In Minecraft, a music pack (part of a Resource Pack) allows players to customize various sound effects in the game, including:

- Background music
- Environmental sounds
- Creature sounds
- Block sounds
- Interface sounds
- And more

By replacing or adding these sound effects, players can enjoy a completely new gaming experience.

## Why Choose MinecraftSounds Music Pack Generator?

### Easy to Use

Our design philosophy is "enabling everyone to create their own music pack." Even if you have no prior knowledge, you can easily create a music pack through our intuitive interface.

### Comprehensive Features

- **Visual Editing**: Drag-and-drop interface, what you see is what you get
- **Sound Preview**: Real-time preview of sound effects
- **Batch Processing**: Support for batch import and editing of sound effects
- **Version Compatibility**: Supports the latest version of Minecraft while being compatible with older versions
- **Project Management**: Easily manage multiple music pack projects

### Open Source and Free

MinecraftSounds is an open-source project, completely free to use. We believe that through community power, this tool can become even more powerful and user-friendly.
